## What changed

First round, at 5e521cf:

- `changeStream` reports each stream's health to a small store (`src/app/stream-health.ts`).
- The conversation and draft polls read only while the chat's stream is down.
- Bootstrap: every 45 seconds while the household stream is down, otherwise once 5 minutes have
  passed since the last good read.
- A catalog that says group chats are unsupported uses the 5-minute fallback.
- Once the household stream or the open chat's has been down for 20 seconds, the existing connection
  notice item shows, with "Reconnecting live updates" when the app and Hermes still answer.

### Review fixes

GPT-6.1 Sol reproduced two cases where data stayed stale with no bound, both of which `main` caught by
polling. The orchestrator decided polls slow down while their stream is live and never stop: the stream
is the fast path and the poll the safety net, so nothing can stay stale without bound, whatever gap is
found next.

- Item 6, the decision: the conversation and the draft are read every 30 seconds while the chat's
  stream is down, as on `main`, and every 2 minutes (`LIVE_POLL_MS`) while it is live. Each poll still
  ticks every 30 seconds and reads once 2 minutes have passed since its last good read, so the worst
  case is 2.5 minutes. Bootstrap keeps 45 seconds down and 5 minutes live.
- Item 5, the missed draft: every conversation read now hands its `draft` to the draft hook, which
  merges it through `mergeDraft`, so an older one changes nothing. A reconnect already reads the
  conversation, so the reconnect's read carries the missed revision at no extra request. Chosen over a
  separate draft read because it is smaller: zero requests instead of one per connection.
- Item 6, the session change: `useConversation` takes the open assistant's `sessionId` from bootstrap.
  When it changes from one session to another, the chat reloads and its stream is closed and reopened,
  since the server's stream state (last sent reply, turn base) followed the old session. A session
  named for the first time is not a change.
- Item 7, the silent replacement: a stream is live only on data at least half a ping interval after
  its headers arrived, the same rule that resets the backoff. Opening, the greeting and the household
  `groups` notice sent at connect never count. The health deadline (two pings plus 5 seconds) is
  separate from the retry backoff, so the 110-second deadline is gone. The first fix measured from the
  request and missed a replacement whose headers took 28 seconds; that fix went to the watchdog branch
  (0bc4adb) and came here by merge.
- Item 8, the notice off the composer: Today, Find, group chats and a chat with no assistant show the
  same connection item at the top of the view, in Today's 720-pixel column. A chat with no assistant
  already shows its own full panel once the connection is lost, so there it adds only the live-updates
  notice. Still one item.
- Item 9: `docs/native-api.md` now says the iOS app reads bootstrap every 45 seconds and the web
  client every 45 seconds while its household stream is down and every 5 minutes while it is live.
  The table above now gives presence and device activity as 15 seconds while attended and recently used.
- Item 10 went to the watchdog branch, which owns the watchdog's rule.
- Today, presence, device activity and the update check are unchanged.

## Request counts

These come from `node .agents/tools/count-stream-requests.mjs`, measured as in the stream open reads
entry. The e2e runtime has no group chats, so its catalog is the unsupported one.

| Scenario | `main` 39e35a6 | Watchdog branch | First round 5e521cf | After the review fixes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold load | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Wake after 70 s hidden | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| Idle chat, 10 min | 113 | 113 | 43 | 51 |
| Idle home, 10 min | 113 | 113 | 43 | 51 |

After the review fixes the idle chat tab made 4 conversation, 4 draft, 2 catalog and 1 bootstrap read,
against 20, 20, 20 and 13 on `main` and 0, 0, 2 and 1 in the first round. It kept 20 presence and 20
device-activity writes, all in the first 5 minutes, since the headless tab had no input after load.
The home tab kept 20 Today reads and 20 device-activity writes; its 4 and 4 are the last chat's hooks.

Why 4 and not 5: each poll ticks every 30 seconds and reads at the first tick at least 2 minutes after
its last good read. The read lands a few milliseconds after its tick, so the tick 2 minutes later is
just short and the next one reads: every 2.5 minutes in practice, which is also the worst case.

The trade, per 10 idle minutes of one open chat, conversation and draft reads together:

| Live interval | Reads | Worst case for a change no stream announces | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| None, as on `main` (30 s always) | 40 | 30 s | measured |
| 1 minute | 12 to 14 | 1.5 min | computed from the tick rule |
| 2 minutes, chosen | 8 | 2.5 min | measured |
| 5 minutes | 2 to 4 | 5.5 min | computed |

2 minutes keeps 62 of the 70 requests the first round saved over `main` in an idle chat, and keeps every
unannounced change under bootstrap's own 5 minutes 45 seconds. The two reproduced gaps no longer
depend on it: both now show at once.

## Connection notice in a blackout

`node .agents/tools/measure-dead-stream.mjs --blackout --blackout-ms 60000` holds every connection,
new ones too, for 60 seconds, then lets the network return.

| | Notice shown after | Notices at once | Cleared after the network returned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `main` 39e35a6 | 42 s | 1 | 22 s |
| First round 5e521cf | 37 s | 1 | 4.8 s |
| After the review fixes | 12 s | 1 | 14.7 s |

All showed "Reconnecting to the app", since bootstrap could not answer either. A screenshot after the
review fixes at 60 seconds showed one notice above the composer. The "Reconnecting live updates" copy,
for a stream that is down while the app answers, is covered by UI tests only.

Both changes come from item 7. The tool cuts 8 seconds after load, before either stream has pinged a
second time, so both still counted as down from connecting and the 20-second threshold passed 12
seconds into the cut. Clearing now waits for the reconnected stream to ping again after its greeting,
plus the jittered retry, instead of clearing on the reconnect's headers. A screenshot of Today, offline,
at 1280 and 390 pixels wide showed the one notice at the top of Today's column.

Dead-stream replacement, `node .agents/tools/measure-dead-stream.mjs`, 2 runs: household 18.0 and
19.0 seconds, chat 18.9 and 18.2, the same as the watchdog branch.

## What shows later than before, and worst cases

- Changes the streams announce show as before, pushed.
- During a silent cut, `main` kept the open chat fresh through its 30-second poll. Here the watchdog
  finds the stream dead at most 25 seconds after its last event and retries 1 to 2 seconds later, and
  the poll reads every 30 seconds while the stream is down.
- Bootstrap-only changes, such as preferences saved on another device, the household list and the iOS
  update notice: from 45 seconds to at most 5 minutes 45 seconds in a tab left in view. Focus or showing
  the page still reads at once.
- A server that gains group chat support: from 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

The review's field table was not in my brief. These are the fields I found that only the conversation
or draft read carries, which had no bound on 5e521cf while the chat stream stayed live:

| Field | 5e521cf, stream live | Now, stream live | `main` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Draft changed while the chat stream reconnected | unbounded | at once, from the reconnect's read | 30 s |
| Draft whose `event: draft` was otherwise lost | unbounded | 2.5 min | 30 s |
| Transcript of a new canonical session | unbounded | at once on the roster notice; 2.5 min if that is missed | 30 s |
| Read position saved on another device, not announced | unbounded | 2.5 min | 30 s |
| Approvals settled outside the app, reconciled on a read | unbounded | 2.5 min | 30 s |
| Any conversation change whose notice was lost | unbounded | 2.5 min | 30 s |
