# November 10 an idle tab polls only what its streams do not announce

Branch `t3/idle-polling`, stacked on `t3/stream-watchdog` at `2e95e56`, which is based on `origin/main`
at `39e35a6`. Checks ran on NicolasLaptop (Fedora, Linux x86_64, Node 24.22.2) in Chromium only. No
real Hermes, iOS build, deployment, Safari, Firefox or phone ran.

## What an idle tab ran on main

| Interval | Where | For | Stream that covers it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conversation read, 30 s | `src/app/use-conversation.ts` `pollWhileVisible(wake, 30_000)` | a chat stream that failed unnoticed | chat stream change notices |
| Draft read, 30 s | `src/app/use-draft.ts` `DRAFT_POLL_MS` | same | chat stream `event: draft` |
| Bootstrap, 45 s | `src/app/use-session.ts` | missed roster notices, changes made outside the app | household `event: roster`, which Hermes outages reach through bot activity; not preferences saved on another device |
| Group catalog, 30 s, or 5 min once the server proved it pushes | `src/App.tsx` | a server that never sends `groups` | household `event: groups` when supported; nothing to watch when unsupported |
| Today, 30 s | `src/components/TodayPanel.tsx` | decisions, scheduled work, results | none |
| Chat presence and device activity, 30 s each | `use-chat-presence.ts`, `use-device-activity.ts` | writes that tell the server who is looking | none; they are writes |
| Household and room stream `open`, 45 s | `use-session.ts`, `GroupChats.tsx` | reopen a refused stream | no request while the stream is live |
| Update check, 30 min | `use-browser.ts` | new release | none; unchanged |

## What changed

- `changeStream` reports each stream's health to a small store (`src/app/stream-health.ts`). A stream
  is down from each connect or failure until an event arrives, and a closed one is not listed. A
  watchdog replacement keeps the outage's start.
- The conversation and draft polls read only while the chat's stream is down. Showing the page still
  reads, because a hidden page closes the stream.
- Bootstrap: every 45 seconds while the household stream is down, otherwise once 5 minutes have
  passed since the last good read.
- A catalog that says group chats are unsupported uses the 5-minute fallback.
- Once the household stream or the open chat's has been down for 20 seconds, the existing connection
  notice item shows. Its new copy, "Reconnecting live updates", applies when the app and Hermes still
  answer. Otherwise the existing offline or unreachable copy shows, in the same single item. It clears
  when the stream hears from the server again.
- Today, presence, device activity and the update check are unchanged.

## Connection notice in a blackout

`node .agents/tools/measure-dead-stream.mjs --blackout --blackout-ms 60000` holds every connection,
new ones too, for 60 seconds, then lets the network return.

| | Notice shown after | Notices at once | Cleared after the network returned |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `main` 39e35a6 | 42 s | 1 | 22 s |
| This branch | 37 s | 1 | 4.8 s |

Both showed "Reconnecting to the app", since bootstrap could not answer either. A screenshot from this
branch at 60 seconds showed one notice above the composer. The "Reconnecting live updates" copy, for a
stream that is down while the app answers, is covered by UI tests only.

## What shows later than before

- Changes the streams announce show as before, pushed.
- During a silent cut, `main` kept the open chat fresh through its 30-second poll. This branch has no
  poll until the watchdog finds the stream dead, at most 25 seconds after its last event, and then
  reconnects and reads. A worst case of 25 seconds plus a reconnect, against 30 seconds before.
- Bootstrap-only changes, such as preferences saved on another device, the household list and the iOS
  update notice: from 45 seconds to at most 5 minutes 45 seconds in a tab left in view. Focus or showing
  the page still reads at once.
- A server that gains group chat support: from 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

## Red proofs

A scratch script broke each line by hand, ran the protecting tests, and restored the source. All 18
mutations went red. One stayed green on the first run: dropping the error report on a failure. The
UI test still passed because the watchdog marks the stream down at 25 seconds, before the 30-second
poll. The health sequence test caught it.

- Polls: the chat poll running while up, or never while down; the draft poll running while up; the chat
  reporting under another name; bootstrap ignoring the household stream, or never falling back while
  live; the household reporting nothing; an unsupported catalog keeping the 30-second poll.
- Notice: ignoring a stream outage; the lost-connection copy for a live-only outage; a replacement
  reporting the stream closed, which made it flash; a 5-second threshold.
- Health: a failure not reported down; an event not reported up; close not forgetting the stream; a
  repeat report restarting the outage; no timer for the threshold; streams not asked about counting.

## Not done

- Presence and device activity remain 30-second writes. The open chat stream could carry presence, but
  that changes the server's presence model.
- Today keeps its 30-second poll, since no stream announces its changes.
- The household and room streams' 45-second `open` timers stay. They send nothing while the stream is
  live, and they reopen a refused stream sooner than the watchdog's backoff.
