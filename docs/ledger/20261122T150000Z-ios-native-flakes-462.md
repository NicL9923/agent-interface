## Large text

Reproduced 5 of 32 runs of the test: 2 of 10 in hold 1 on unchanged code, then 3 of 22 on copies that add only logging to the audit handler. Every failure is an audit issue on Settings with no element and type Dynamic Type: "unsupported" or "partially unsupported". The Close button's two issues were reported in every one of the 1,440 Settings audits of the probe runs below, so an unbound Close button is not the cause. The two element-less issues always come together in the same audit, nearly always the first audit after Settings opens.

30 of 320 launches. The covered home screen, leftovers from the home audit, the account badge, the account text, the profile menu and settling time are ruled out as the sole cause. Allowing the pair in the audit's exception list would weaken the test, so that is a decision, not a fix.
