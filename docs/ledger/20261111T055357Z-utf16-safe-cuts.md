## What main did

Each of these cut text at a UTF-16 index with `slice`, which can fall inside an emoji's surrogate pair, a flag, a family emoji or a
letter and its accent, leaving half of what a person sees as one character:

- `spokenText` and `spokenProgress` (`talk.ts`) cut the head at the limit, then, with no space in it, at `Math.max(lastIndexOf(" "), 1)`.
  For Chinese, Japanese or a run of emoji that is index 1: the voice said the first character and "The rest is in the chat." The
  head's own cut at the limit could also split a pair.
- `clean` (`talk.ts`), which shortens the assistant's name and description for the voice's instructions.
- `previewOf` (`group-latest.ts`), the home preview of a room's newest message, at 159 units before the ellipsis.
- `excerpt` (`event-copy.ts`), at its 60% point when no space reaches back that far.

## Change

`safeCut(text, index)` is the largest index at or below `index` on a grapheme boundary, by `Intl.Segmenter` where the runtime has it
(Node and every current browser do), else on a code-point boundary. `cutText(text, limit)` is the text up to it. Limits stay counts of
UTF-16 units, as `String.length` counts, and stay upper bounds. The segmenter looks 64 units past the limit; a character longer than
that starting the text is cut at a code point rather than emptying the text.

## Decisions

- Grapheme over code point: a flag cut between its two regional indicators, or a family emoji between joiners, is a code point cut and
  still reads as a broken character. The code-point fallback exists for a runtime without `Intl.Segmenter` and is tested with it removed.
- With no space in the head, `spokenText` and `spokenProgress` now say the head, not its first character. `Math.max(…, 1)` guarded
  `slice(0, -1)`; it was not a design. This is a behavior change for text with no spaces, in the same lines.
- `clean` and `excerpt` are the same defect in the same files, so they use the helper too. `excerpt` needs only its 60% point: its head cut
  is followed by a cut at a space or at that point, so the head's own end never survives.
- Not changed: `clean` and `spokenProgress` replace control and format characters, including the zero-width joiner, with spaces, so
  a family emoji reaches the voice as four emoji. That is the existing sanitizer, and speech does not read it.

## Review fix (Claude Opus 5.5 on `2b575ad`)

P3, `src/server/talk.ts`: `spokenText` and `spokenProgress` cut at the last space wherever it fell, so a Chinese reply with one
English word near its start, such as "OK 今日は…", was spoken as "OK" and a pointer to the chat. A space now counts only at or
past 60% of the head, as in `excerpt`; otherwise the cut is at the limit. `。`, `！` and `？` are sentence ends in both, with the
same position rule as `.`, `!` and `?`, so they end a late sentence and an early one is passed over. Tests cover a
late and an early space on both sides of the boundary, each of the three marks late and early, and the English-word case for
both functions.
